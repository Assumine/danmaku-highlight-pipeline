# Contributing

Changes to detection rules should include evidence from labeled samples and focused tests. Please describe:

- the room or content type used for calibration;
- whether the change affects discovery, start time, end time, or rendering;
- false positives and false negatives before and after the change;
- any new expressions or emote IDs and why they represent the claimed reaction.

Do not submit raw credentials, non-anonymized viewer identifiers, or copyrighted video samples without permission.
